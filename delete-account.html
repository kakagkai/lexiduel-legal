Delete your Lexiduel data

Lexiduel · developer: kakagkai - studio · effective 9 November 2026

Summary. Lexiduel has no account, email or password. Your data lives in two places: on your device and, if you played online, as one small record on our server. You can delete the device data yourself. For the server record, email us.

1. Delete data on your device

In the app open Settings → Account → Delete all data. This removes your progress, coins, settings and the server token from your device. Uninstalling the app also removes local data.

2. Delete your server record

If you played online, adding friends or buying “remove ads”, our server holds a record: a random install token, your display name, your rating, games played and wins, your friend codes and friend list, and whether you bought “remove ads”. It holds no email address, real name, location or game history.

To have it deleted, email support.kakagkai.studio@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Lexiduel data” and tell us:

  • your display name in the game;
  • your friend code (shown in the friends section of the app), if you still have it;
  • roughly when you last played online.

We delete the record, including your friend connections, within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Tip: send the request before using “Delete all data” in the app. That button also removes the token that identifies you to the server, so afterwards we can only find your record from your name and friend code. If we cannot identify it, it is removed automatically 180 days after your last online session.

3. What we cannot delete

Google keeps the record of your Google Play purchase under its own privacy policy. Ask Google to manage or delete it.

4. Contact

Privacy questions: support.kakagkai.studio@gmail.com. See also the Privacy Policy, sections 5, 9 and 11.
