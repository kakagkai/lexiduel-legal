Privacy Policy

LexiDuel · effective 18 October 2026 · applies to the Android app and the website

Summary. The game itself is offline: your progress, coins and settings never leave your device unless you play online or add a friend. Online play stores a display name and a score on our server; adding a friend stores the fact that you two are friends. Advertising is served by Google, which may use the device advertising identifier. We do not run analytics, we do not sell anything about you, and a friend learns that you played a word - nothing more.

1. What this app is

LexiDuel is a word game. Played on its own it is entirely local: the game is one HTML file, it works with no network connection, and your progress, coins, unlocks and settings live in your device's own storage. Nothing is uploaded.

If you connect to our servers - online duels, tournaments or the leaderboard - then the data in section 3 applies.

2. Intended audience

This game is rated for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13, and if we learn that we have, we delete it.

We do not use a child-directed advertising configuration, and we do not serve personalised advertising to users we have identified as under 13.

3.1 Friends, and what a friend can see

Friendship is mutual and deliberate. Adding a friend needs both of you: the first request gives neither of you any visibility, and only when the other player accepts does anything become visible. Neither of you can watch the other alone.

Your friend code is a short handle derived from your install token by a one-way hash. It is not your token and it grants nobody access to your account - it is only a way to be found. You choose who to give it to.

When friendship exists, a friend is told that you finished a word: your display name, whether you won, the mode, and the language. We send at most a couple of those notices per friend per day so the game never feels like it is reporting on you. Your guesses, the words themselves, the time you took, and your rating change are never sent.

We do not keep a log of your games, a history of whom you played, or a record of when you played, on disk. The weekly scoreboard shows one number per friend: how far their rating has moved since Monday.

4. Advertising

The app is supported by ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and use:

  • the device advertising identifier;
  • your IP address;
  • interactions with an ad, such as whether you watched or tapped it;
  • diagnostic information such as device model and app version.

Consent for advertising is asked for inside the app, before any ad is shown. Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are also shown Google's consent form (the Google User Messaging Platform, a consent management platform certified by Google), which passes their choice to Google in the standard format.

You can withdraw consent at any time from Settings → Privacy Policy → Withdraw consent. In the regions above, Settings → Privacy Policy → Ad privacy reopens Google's form so you can change your choice there. You can also opt out of personalised ads in Google's Ad Settings.

Buying the one-off "remove ads" purchase suppresses advertising for you permanently, on that install and on any future install of the same purchase.

5. Your choices

  • Play offline. No connection means no data leaves your device. Every mode except online duels works this way, and so does every mode if you decline consent.
  • Be unlisted. Nobody can find you by friend code unless you gave them the code, and a one-sided request shows them nothing about you.
  • Erase everything. Settings → Account → Erase all data removes your local progress, your server token and your cached entitlement from the device. To have your server-side record removed too, contact us at the address below; we delete it within 30 days.
  • Withdraw ad consent. Settings → Privacy Policy → Withdraw consent, at any time, including after you have already played. The game then stays offline and ad-free until you consent again.

6. Legal bases (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies: consent for advertising; performance of a contract for online play, friendships and purchases; and legitimate interests for keeping the service secure and the ladder ordered. You may withdraw consent at any time, and object to processing based on legitimate interests.

7. Sharing

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for anyone else's benefit. Three things are visible to someone other than us:

  • Google, as needed to deliver ads (section 4) and to process your purchase (section 3), under Google's own privacy policy.
  • A friend you accepted, and only the notice described in section 3.1 - never your guesses, never your words, never your rating change.
  • Nobody else. We would disclose data if legally required, and would tell you unless prohibited from doing so.

8. Children and families

The game is not directed at under-13s and we do not knowingly collect their data. We do not participate in child-directed advertising programmes, we do not use a families configuration, and we do not request age in the app.

9. Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and objection. Contact us and we will act within 30 days. If you are in the EEA, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Data we do not collect

We do not collect: email addresses, real names, phone numbers, contacts, photos, precise or coarse location, microphone or camera input, or health data. We do not run an analytics service and we do not run a crash-reporting service; diagnostics you see in your device's system log stay on your device.

What we keep from your play is aggregate: games played and wins. We do not keep a history of individual games, the words you guessed, how long a round took you, or a log of when you were online. The weekly scoreboard stores one extra number per player - the rating your week started from - so it can show movement rather than a lifetime total.

11. How long we keep it, and what happens if we get busy

Your record is kept until you ask us to delete it, or until 180 days after your last session. That is also how long a friend edge survives if neither of you ever returns.

One honest caveat: the server caps the number of player records it keeps. If that cap is ever reached, we drop the records that have been idle longest, before their 180 days are up. Nothing about you is sold or handed over in that case - the record is simply gone, and you start fresh on your next visit.

Data in transit is encrypted. Our server may be hosted outside your country, including in the United States; where that happens, transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
